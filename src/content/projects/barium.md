A browser-based tool for building and sharing 4D geospatial scenes — no coding required. Draw points, lines, polygons, circles, and rectangles directly on a CesiumJS globe, import existing data from GeoJSON, KML, GPX, CSV, and CZML, upload `.glb` / `.gltf` models and custom globe layers, then use the time-aware tooling to animate objects along paths and play the whole scenario back. Scenes are stored in the cloud, organized into folders and layers, and shareable with a link that works on mobile and desktop.

Developed alongside [Kevin Ten Eyck](https://github.com/kteneyck).
